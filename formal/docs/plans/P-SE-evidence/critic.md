## unmentioned constraints
- CORRECTION to the survey claim that Lean-vs-Yul equivalence needs no x-not-in-domain side condition (R-2, first manifestation). When x^n = 1, Lagrange.yul:93-99 reverts with ERR_PROOF_REJECTED: batch_invert of (x - omega_i) and of x^n - 1 sees a zero. Lean's lIRange (Domain.lean:251-257) silently returns zeros and raises no Fault. Take x = omega^j with j outside the rotation window. Then every Lean l_r is correct (0), the quotient term is multiplied by x^n - 1 = 0, and an honest proof is accepted by Lean (and by Rust) but rejected by the contract. Because lowering_equivalence quantifies over all H, such an H exists. So the completeness half of lowering_equivalence, the iff in lowering_equivalence_noAcc and end_to_end_noAcc all need an explicit hypothesis `x ^ vk.n ≠ 1` on the squeezed x, or ContractRef.accepts must include the domain check. The other manifestations are consistent across the two sides. If x3 hits a point-set point, the contract's scalar_inv reverts (AssemblyHelpers.yul:22-23) and Lean panics at kzg/mod.rs:479. For logup.rs:460, Lean panics (Logup.lean:279); the contract's behaviour there is unverified.
- FieldConsts on the verifier path matters only through numBits. truncate (Arith.lean:283-286) keeps divCeil (divCeil numBits 8) 2 low LE bytes, while the Yul masks to 128 bits (truncMask128, Kzg.lean:394; the x3 squeeze in TranscriptProofParser.yul). transcriptParser_spec and pcs_spec therefore need `divCeil (divCeil E.field.numBits 8) 2 = 16` (for example numBits = 255). rootOfUnity, zeta and s are read only by EvaluationDomain.new (Domain.lean:135-145), which the verifier never calls because vk.domain is imported.
